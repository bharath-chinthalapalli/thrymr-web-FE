#### Project Overview

Thrymr enabled digital transformation initiatives for a leading wellness firm that gives its clients nutrition counseling and built digital wellness center capabilities to deliver a smooth, personalized customer experience and coordinate marketing activities across channels.

##### Implementation

<!-- <span style="color:red"> December 2019</span> -->

#### Problem Statement

- The wellness firm established brick-and-mortar centers and they were independently managed with limited communication and interactivity among them leading to lot of inefficiencies.
- The firm undertook a digital transformation journey to build a more scalable business model and needed a mature technology partner to achieve the goals.
- The firm wanted to improve customer satisfaction and establish a differentiated brand by providing superior online and cross- channel customer experience.

#### Solution & implementation

- Thrymr designed, developed and deployed an integrated digital platform that consolidated all digital marketing content, loyalty membership applications to coordinate promotional activities.
- The platform integrates with O2O (on-line to off-line) e-commerce and consumer service platform to compile data from the entire customer journey.
- Thrymr business analysts led the client C-level executives to articulate innovative and high-value customer journeys Built a robust, easy-to-use digital wellness center platform on top of Java technologies.

#### Results

- User-centered weight management that ensured 1000% increase in user base in less than 2 years.
- Integrated solution that delivered measurable outcomes at user scale, reducing costs and increasing productivity. Substantial lower operation costs that were passed to the user through discounts and rewards.
- Simple design and powerful engagement led to a 600% increase in in-house product sales.
- Empowered application to stream patients to the right dietician, improve patient communications and optimize appointment slots effectively. The same number of dieticians now manage 10 times more the users.
