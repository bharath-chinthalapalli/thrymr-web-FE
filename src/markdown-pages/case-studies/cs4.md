#### Project Overview

A Leading innovative digital platform firm specializes in trading businesses and commercial properties online engaged Thrymr to boost their online capabilities and this collaboration has resulted in a sophisticated platform that is helping the client to drive revenue growth, improved brand visibility, cost efficiencies and a greatly enhanced customer functions experience for all stakeholders of the platform.

<!-- <span style="color:red"> _Implementation-January 2019_</span> -->

#### Problem Statement

- Our client engaged Thrymr to help rethink its digital platform, prepare for a mobile world, and take an innovative approach to provide an online customer experience to all stakeholders.
- The client envisaged to build a well thought out digital platform that provides superior analytics, personalization and customer engagement capabilities with a global reach.
- The engagement objectives included the migration of pre-existing static public-web content, data and formats to the new platform and enhancement of transaction capabilities for all stakeholders namely buyers, sellers, franchisors, business brokers and business service providers.

#### Solution & Implementation

- Thrymr worked closely with the client’s CIO organization to develop a digital platform strategy and an architectural strategy and presented it to the CIO team.
- Thrymr leveraged its own content management platform ‘Northern lights’ to consolidate client’s numerous websites onto a single platform and the platform is hosted on ‘Digital Ocean’ in an infrastructure-as-a-service capacity.
- Thrymrs’ team established a practical Agile Engineering approach and Continuous Development approach and collaborated with the clients’ team and ensured a cost-effective and smooth implementation.

#### Results

-The consolidation of multiple websites into a single digital platform and the cloud-based approach to implementation lowered the maintenance effort and cost significantly.
-The analytics-driven ability and new mobile-based tools provided personalized content and easy-to-use journeys and this has resulted in better stakeholder experience.
-The new platform is positioning our client to make continual improvements to the business model and fine-tune platform with greater speed-to-market and sustain high levels of performance in its online business.
